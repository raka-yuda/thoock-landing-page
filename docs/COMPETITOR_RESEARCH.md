# Competitor research for Thoock's purchase flow

Reviewed 27 October 2026. Public pages and advertised plans only; no accounts, transactions, or product-quality benchmarks. Marketing claims and testimonials are not independently verified. Prices can change and different currencies/usage units are not directly comparable.

## Recommendation for Thoock

Keep the existing visual identity. Lead with the microstock workflow and use real screens to demonstrate the value of keeping opportunities, briefs, images, review decisions and exports connected.

Metadata, batch processing, multi-platform exports and research are not individually unique. The connected desktop experience is a positioning hypothesis that needs demonstration and customer validation.

Thoock currently requires activation and has no usable demo or trial. The public funnel should be:

1. Understand the workflow from the hero and real app screens.
2. Evaluate it through the recorded walkthrough and an inspectable example export.
3. Review the price, license scope, supported OS/architecture and BYOK requirements.
4. Buy through the payment service.
5. Receive the key, download the correct installer and activate.

Use “Get Thoock” with the current price in the hero, linking to pricing. Use “Watch the walkthrough” as the secondary action. The pricing action should explicitly say “Buy lifetime license.” Keep “Already have a license? Download Thoock” for existing customers. A recorded walkthrough must not be described as an interactive demo.

Suggested hero copy:

> Your microstock workflow. All together.
>
> Research opportunities, shape briefs, generate or import images, review results, and prepare export packages in one desktop workspace.
>
> Get Thoock — [live price] · Watch the walkthrough
>
> One-time license required to activate. Bring your own AI keys; provider usage is billed separately.

A small example batch can demonstrate one opportunity, its brief, a reviewed image, generated metadata and the resulting export files. This is evidence visitors can inspect before purchasing, without claiming a trial exists. Use owned sample files and remove private paths/credentials.

Describe the local workspace accurately: project storage is local; enabled external AI operations may send relevant content to the selected provider. Avoid blanket “everything stays on your computer” claims.

Do not promise higher earnings, acceptance rates or superior metadata without measurements. Do not imply that “lifetime” includes unlimited AI usage. Define license duration, major-version updates, devices and refund terms before presenting those as benefits.

Treat the current price as a hypothesis. The reviewed alternatives bundle different services and AI costs, so their sticker prices do not justify an automatic price cut. Measure pricing views, walkthrough engagement, checkout starts and server-confirmed paid orders separately. Downloads after payment are delivery events, not the main acquisition goal.
